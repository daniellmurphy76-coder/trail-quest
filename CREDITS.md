## Changes made to the models

The models are CC0, so none of this is required, but it is recorded here. Unused animations and tangent data were removed; material colors were baked into vertex colors and meshes merged to cut draw calls; the animal and cabin meshes were simplified or quantized to fit the size budget; some models were turned to face +z and re-centered; and four City Kit (Commercial) buildings had their wall color changed. Scale and ground offset for each model live in `public/assets/manifest.json`.

The nature art was replaced on 2026-10-03 because the Kenney Nature Kit paints every leaf mint-teal, which clashed with the grass green, the salmon tents, and the grey rocks. What was done to build the new one (all of it scripted in `scripts/assets-build-nature.mjs`, run from the unmodified downloads):

- **KayKit models:** the pack's single 1024 px gradient atlas was baked into vertex colors (a green, a trunk-brown, and a stone ramp), so the shipped GLBs have no texture and no UVs. The ramps keep KayKit's light ends; the dark end of the green is pulled from emerald toward leaf green, the trunk orange is a little browner, and the blue-grey stone is warmed slightly. The autumn tree swaps the leaf ramp for an autumn one, the birch swaps the trunk ramp for a white one. The wide pine, the squashed boulders, and the flat rock are non-uniform stretches of the pack's meshes. Then the meshes were welded, deduplicated, and quantized (KHR_mesh_quantization). Nothing else was altered.
- **Original models:** flowers, mushrooms, the stump, logs, and the flagstone floor are built in code from simple shapes, painted from the same ramps.
- **Kenney leftovers:** the pastel peach, salmon, and mint vertex colors of the tents, fences, bridge, and path pieces were swapped one for one (salmon to terracotta, peach to wood tones, mint to grass green). The City Kit color maps were nudged: saturation 90 percent, greens 40 percent of the way from mint toward leaf green, and a faint warm cast on the greys. The untouched originals are not kept in the repo; `scripts/assets-build-nature.mjs` re-applies the change to a fresh download.
