## Changes made to the models

The models are CC0, so none of this is required, but it is recorded here. Unused animations and tangent data were removed; material colors were baked into vertex colors and meshes merged to cut draw calls; the animal and cabin meshes were simplified or quantized to fit the size budget; some models were turned to face +z and re-centered; and four City Kit (Commercial) buildings had their wall color changed. Nothing else was altered. Scale and ground offset for each model live in `public/assets/manifest.json`.
